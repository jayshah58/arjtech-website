Services

Zero Trust & Security Architecture

Zero Trust, designed in from the start. Many organizations know they need Zero Trust but struggle to turn the idea into a design they can actually build. ARJTECH produces a practical, phased target-state architecture aligned to NIST 800-207, with segmentation mapped to your real application tiers and compliance obligations.

What you get

  • A Zero Trust readiness assessment and phased roadmap
  • Segmentation policy matrices and enforcement plans
  • Security control patterns and guardrails
  • Alignment of logging and incident response with your SOC and NOC

Who it’s for

Regulated organizations starting, or restarting, a Zero Trust program.

Experience

Designed Zero Trust micro-segmentation mapped to NIST 800-207 across the critical infrastructure of a federal financial institution.

Popular starting point

Zero Trust Readiness Assessment

Current-state findings, a target state and a phased roadmap.

Firewall & Perimeter Security

Modernize your perimeter without disrupting production. Replacing firewalls is one of the riskiest changes an IT team makes. ARJTECH plans and delivers firewall and edge migrations with parallel runs and validated rollback, so the business keeps running while the platform changes underneath it.

What you get

  • Firewall architecture, from HA clusters to elastic firewall fabrics
  • Phased migration with parallel run and tested rollback
  • Dual-ISP internet edge design
  • TLS, cipher and certificate lifecycle standards

Who it’s for

Organizations replacing end-of-life or vendor-managed firewalls, or consolidating across datacenters.

Experience

Replaced vendor-managed firewalls with internally run Cisco FTD clusters across three datacenters, cutting change turnaround from 10+ business days to 2–3 days. Delivered an elastic Check Point Maestro fabric with Cisco APIC integration and multi-tenant policy.

Firewall Modernization Roadmap

Platform options, a migration plan and a rollback plan.

Hybrid Cloud & Azure Networking

Extend your network into Azure, not your risk. Moving production workloads to Azure shouldn’t create a new, weaker trust boundary. ARJTECH designs hybrid connectivity that behaves like a natural extension of your private network, with tested failover and clear governance.

What you get

  • Hybrid connectivity using ExpressRoute, DMVPN and BGP
  • Failover and convergence testing
  • Security and routing governance
  • Operational runbooks for your team

Who it’s for

Organizations moving production workloads into Azure, or fixing a hybrid design that grew without a plan.

Experience

Extended an encrypted WAN into two Azure regions over MPLS and internet, with redundant hubs and dual-transport failover. Backed by the Microsoft Azure Solutions Architect Expert (AZ-305) certification.

Datacenter & WAN Modernization

Simpler infrastructure that costs less to run. Datacenter fabrics and WAN edges tend to sprawl, driving up hardware, power and support costs. ARJTECH assesses what you have, designs a simpler target architecture and plans the move without putting operations at risk.

What you get

  • Current-state assessment
  • Target architecture: Cisco ACI multipod, tenant and VRF isolation, DR design
  • Local internet breakout with secure web gateway
  • Migration and cutover plan

Who it’s for

Organizations with sprawling fabrics, rising hardware and power costs, or branches backhauling all traffic.

Experience

Consolidated isolated datacenter fabrics into a tenant-based multipod design across two active sites with 400 Gbps inter-pod links and an independent DR site, reducing hardware and power.

Datacenter Modernization Assessment

Current state, target architecture and a phased plan.

About

About ARJTECH

ARJTECH INC. is an architecture and advisory firm based in the Calgary Metropolitan Area that designs secure network, security and cloud infrastructure for organizations that cannot afford failure. We serve enterprise and mid-market organizations, especially in regulated and critical-infrastructure sectors, as a project-based or fractional architecture partner.

We are deliberately senior and deliberately practical. We deliver quickly, document thoroughly and hand over something your team can actually operate. When a project needs more hands or a different specialty, we bring in qualified partners and stay accountable for the result.
